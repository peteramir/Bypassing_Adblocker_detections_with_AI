Permissions and limits
  • Access to all sites (required): lets the extension inject its reader into the tab and its frames, re-request script files across origins, and watch request metadata. Request headers, cookies and bodies are not recorded.
  • webRequest (required): observe-only. Records URL, type, status, content-type and errors for scripts and similar requests, in memory and session storage.
  • Debugger (optional, Deep capture only): requested when you tick the box.
  • Nothing is uploaded. The ZIP is built in the browser and saved by you.
  • Not all sources are reachable: closed shadow roots, sandboxed frames, revoked blob: URLs and requests made before monitoring began are reported as limitations in the ZIP.
